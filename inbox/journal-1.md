# I miss being loved
#journal #love #longing #thoughts

I miss being loved. I miss how my mother hugs me, I miss how my father grasps my hand when we're walking together,
I miss waking up to their voice doing chores around the house, I miss being loved by a woman, I miss the days I woke up smiling because I knew
I have them in my life.

Now that I'm oceans away from my parents and no contact from with her. Sometimes I wake up in the morning dreaming of just an ordinary day around them, doing unsignificant things but they mean so much to me now. Just making a coffee while my mom is doing the dishes or going up to the balcony for some fresh air
while my dad is working, sipping his tea. Coming home to call my girlfriend to talk about an interesting leaf I saw on my way to school or talk about how cold
the weather is. It's not the grand events or the flashy memories you miss the most, it's the mundane, the background stuff. How they sit at the couch or how
they look out the window with the same gaze they always have. Those moments makes you feel at ease, brings you peace knowing everything is alright and any
worries will be okay as long as you have them. You miss their subconcious stuff, where they place their mug everytime, how they smell after they wake up,
the groans they make while getting up, the way they scrunch their eyes to focus on the text they're reading, how evening sun bounces off their skin, their
little weird obsession with a pen, how they leave the spoon by the coffee machine to use it next time...

It's not that they don't love me, I know they do. It is about feeling it. Pixels on a screen or a sound from the speaker of my phone don't give me the same
warmth as their voice or gaze. I can't see the wrinkles that form everytime they smile. I can't hear their heartbeat. I just wish everyone I love was 5 minutes away from me
at all times. Such a childish wish but it's truly what heart desires. But then again, if I never feel their absence, can't deal with the distance, then I would never
realise how much their love means to be at times like these. Love genuinely feels the strongest when there's distance in between.
